Discover The Labnut's 4 Textures

Every jar encloses 100% pure Greek almonds, without any additives. Find the texture that suits you!

1. The Labnut Smooth

Texture: Silky & Runny (Whole grain)

Our classic recipe. The almonds are milled with their skin until a silky spread is created. Ideal to pour over pancakes, into smoothies, or your yogurt.

2. The Labnut Thick

Texture: Rich & Spreadable (Whole grain)

For those who love a more stable spread. It has a full body and doesn't run easily, making it the perfect choice to spread generously on a warm slice of bread.

3. The Labnut Crunchy

Texture: Runny with crunchy bits (Whole grain)

The perfect combination. Our soft runny base meets premium, roasted almond pieces to offer you a unique crunchy experience in every bite.

4. The Labnut White

Texture: Velvety (From blanched almonds)

Our most refined flavor. Produced exclusively from skinless almonds. It has a light color, incredibly velvety texture, and a slightly sweeter, milder taste.
